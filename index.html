Privacy Policy — Langırt: The Foosball Game

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Langırt: The Foosball Game ("the game") does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The game has no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or third-party tracking.
  • Settings, progress and club/cup edits are stored only on your device.
  • An image you pick from your gallery for a club or cup crest is scaled down and saved only in the game's own storage on your device; it is never uploaded.
  • If you enter an image address (https://…) for a crest, the game downloads the image directly from that address. The request goes only to the address you entered; no data reaches the developer. The image stays on your device.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all of its data from your device.
